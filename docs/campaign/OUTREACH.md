# Outreach messages

Short, human, one ask each. The offer is the same everywhere: a free ACT level check, no signup, that tells a student where to start. Never promise score gains. Never mention the founder's age. Send one at a time, from your own account, to people you have a reason to write to; do not mass-send or paste the same text to a list.

Rules for every message:
- Say what it is in one line and what it costs (nothing, no signup).
- Offer; do not push. One follow-up at most, a week later, only if they replied or opened it.
- Savant is in testing: say so. Do not claim results.
- Use the audience's own link from TRACKING-LINKS.md (`ref=outreach-<audience>`).

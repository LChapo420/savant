## The formats
**A. Absurd what-if, about 40 s.** One impossible image, shown as if real. Each scene is stranger.
**B. Character soap, 60 to 90 s, labelled "Part 1".** A lovable non-human underdog is humiliated, a secret or reversal builds, and it ends on a cliffhanger. Faces show big emotion. Hook: "Everyone laughed at X, until..."
**C. Short POV, about 20 s.** One feeling everyone knows, with a sting at the end.

## Review as a virality analyst
- **Strongest evidence:** Absurd what-if from a tiny account (2.9M with 2,929 followers) is the closest proof that a new account can do this. Character soap has the highest repeat numbers (5.3M, 5.0M).
- **Best bets, my judgment:** Math 1, Brain 1, Study 1, School 2, Math 3, Brain 2. Math 1 has a real twist: Zero matters next to 1.
- **Biggest risk: the wrong audience.** Fruit soap viewers came for drama. A viral video may bring followers who never apply. These ideas stay inside school and learning, so the audience is closer, but this is untested. Watch applicants per view, not only views.
- **Unproven:** none of these exact ideas has been tested, and I found no AI school story at 1M. These videos are AI-labelled, and a platform may treat that differently over time.
- **Watch the proof first:** I read captions and numbers but didn't watch these. Look at thawhatifguy and fruitsoapai before generating.
- **Parts:** only make Part 2 of what gets traction. Content factory can make 1 to 4 episodes, so don't pay for all 4 up front.
- **Credits:** 40 s to 80 s runs about 40 to 85 credits per video. Check your balance.

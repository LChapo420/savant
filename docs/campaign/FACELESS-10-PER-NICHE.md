## What the winners share
1. **Length:** most are 60 to 70 s. TikTok's Creator Rewards pays only for videos over a minute, which may explain why so many creators aim there; check TikTok's current rules. The one big exception is A at 13 s.
2. **Opens on you:** "Why you..." or "Why smart people...". It's about the viewer, so people see themselves in it and send it to friends. This is my reading, consistent with Berger & Milkman 2012 on sharing and Loewenstein 1994 on curiosity.
3. **Answers something you've noticed but can't explain.**
4. **Flatters:** "...mean you're smart" (B, C, E). It works, but it's also where these videos get loose with facts, so ours state only what the science supports.
5. **Look:** dark background, one glowing subject, big words.
6. **Interactive:** "count the F's" and "what's your answer?" keep people watching and commenting.

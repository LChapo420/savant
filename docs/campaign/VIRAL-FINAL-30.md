# The 30 for tonight, re-ranked from measured views (5 Nov 2026)

Replaces the top 6 lists in VIRAL-TOP-10.md. Keep that file for hooks, structure and flop risks.

**What this is.** Public YouTube view counts, read through search metadata on 5 Nov: 59 searches on the ideas, then 25 "what if ..." searches, keeping 66 what-if videos of 3 minutes or less from 53 channels. YouTube only: TikTok and Instagram were not measured.

**Limits.**
- Survivorship: search favours popular videos, so these are the winners. The sample median of 202k is not what a new video should expect.
- Most top videos are years old and from established channels. Views have built up over time. Followers were not read.
- A new account can get nothing with the same format: "What If Humans Didn't Need Sleep?" from The What If Lab had 40 views.
- The first run of 59 searches was noisy (unrelated videos ranked top, for example "Honey in space"), so its top videos are not evidence. Used only where a title truly matches.
- No video can be shown to be certain to go viral.

## What the measured winners share

| Pattern | Real examples (views) |
|---|---|
| A huge, physical, everyone-affected event | What if the Moon Crashed into the Earth? 75.4M; What If The Earth Were Twice As Big? 13.5M; What Would Happen If The World Lost Oxygen For 5 Seconds? 11.2M; What if the Sun disappeared? 7.8M; What Would Happen if Humans Disappeared? 4.9M |
| "What if X disappeared" | What if Teachers Disappeared? (compilation, 14 min) 1.4M; What if Number 0 Disappeared? (compilation, 3.7 min) 3.2M; What if Numbers Disappeared? 671k; What if Gravity Disappeared? 213k |
| A strange image you want to see | what if animals were round? 67.8M; What if wild animals ate fast food 12.6M |
| Existing fandoms | Hogwarts, Gravity Falls, Roblox, FNAF: millions, but we can't use other people's characters |
| Absurdly specific | What if we put a pool on the moon? 2.0M |

**What did not show up with big numbers in this sample:** study tips, psychology facts, myth-busting, and named math puzzles. Birthday paradox top result 789, 0.999 = 1 top 3k, Monty Hall top 2k, fold paper top 2k, "math facts" top 483, "psychology facts" top 58, "study smarter" top 64. Treat that as a warning, not proof: small searches, mostly small channels.

**Length.** Winners in this sample run 41 to 170 seconds, longer than the 15 to 30 s planned. Not proof either, because the sample allowed up to 3 minutes and older videos were often longer. A 1-minute video costs about 63 credits against about 16 for 15 s.

## The 30

Evidence: **M** a matching title has real views; **P** the pattern is measured, this exact idea is not; **J** judgment only.

# Polish pass, learner app, 2026-10-24

Beta polish of the learner-facing app (`public/`): design consistency with v2 "Measured and marked", accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) and speed. I changed only how things look, read and load, never what a feature does. Anything bigger is staged in `staged/polish-2026-10-24/NOTE.md`.

## How it was audited

- **Where:**
  - the placement-uitest server on port 6799 (`scratchpad/.../polish/polish-server.js`), running the live app tree;
  - a scratchpad copy of `db.uitest.json`;
  - every model key blanked, `OLLAMA_ENABLED=0`, and the round-3 and skills round-3 flags on.
  - Never the live server, never `data/db.json`.
- **Test learners:** the round-3 and skills round-3 seed scripts added test learners to the DB copy, so the new screens could be reached. These cover review, consolidation, remediation, held graduation, too-fast, chess, piano and basketball stages, and the stage gate.
- **When:** after the apply-live agent's steps 1-2 landed (round 3 01-05 at 22:02, skills round 3 01-04 at 22:15, chess links at about 22:50 local).
- **Tool:** headless Chrome over CDP (`polish/audit.js`), at 375, 768 and 1280 px, light and dark, for **53 page states**. That is 318 views before and the same 318 after.
- **Page states covered:**
  - **Static pages (40):** landing, sign-in, register, privacy, the 4 parent pages, dashboard, profile, onboarding, curriculum, style quiz, survey, subject placement, placement, challenge exam, base lesson, help, and 7 path variants (math, English, review, consolidation, held, chess, piano, basketball).
  - **Player and new screens:** the player, review, held, stage check, skill practice, stage left, and the 3 chess pages.
  - **Interactive flows (13):** player verdict, placement result, review question, verdict and done, held recap, held page, too-fast note, stage sitting and result, practice set, and basketball count.
- **What each view checks:**
  - a full-page screenshot;
  - horizontal overflow;
  - console errors;
  - text contrast, computed from the rendered colours, OKLCH included;
  - unnamed controls (from the accessibility tree);
  - unlabelled fields, heading order and h1, and target size;
  - a 40-step Tab walk: ring present, focused control visible and not covered by a sticky or fixed bar (2.4.11);
  - the reduced-motion state;
  - every network request, with size and time.
- **Before/after screenshots:** `curriculum-build/polish-2026-10-24/` (12 pairs).
- **Backups:**
  - `scratchpad/.../before-polish/public/` is a snapshot of html/css/js taken after the patches landed;
  - `before-polish/at-edit/` holds each file as it was just before I edited it (see `at-edit.log`).

## What was fixed

### Speed
1. **Fonts downloaded four times** (`fonts/fonts.css`).
   - Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400/500/600/700 are byte-identical copies of one variable file, and Mono 400/500/700 likewise. Each weight was its own `@font-face`, so a page fetched the same 34 KB file up to 4 times, plus Mono up to 3 times.
   - Now there is one face per family with `font-weight: 400 700`, pointing at the -400 file.
   - Rendering is unchanged: a pixel diff of the landing page body at 1280 showed 0 changed pixels outside the header.
   - The other font files are left in place.
2. **No favicon.**
   - Every page load asked for `/favicon.ico`. The catch-all answered with the 5.8 KB landing HTML, as a 200.
   - Added `favicon.svg`: the three-tick mark, ink, with a dark-mode fill.
   - Linked from all 29 pages.
3. **Profile fetched each subject's skills one after another** (`js/profile.js`): 9 enrollments meant 9 round trips in series.
   - Now they are fetched together and drawn in the same order.
   - On localhost that is about 120 ms in series; over a real connection it is roughly one round trip per subject.

### Layout
4. **Landing had no side gutter from 721 to 1168 px** (`css/style.css`, section 19).
   - `.hero`, `.landing-section`, `.cta-end` and `.site-footer` set `padding: X 0`, which wiped `.wrap`'s side padding. Text ran to the screen edge, and the week scale's "35" was cut off (the 772 px scroll at 768).
   - They now set `padding-block` only.
5. **Landing demo indented 40 px** from every other left edge: the browser's default `<figure>` margin. Set to `margin: 0`.
6. **Held page scrolled sideways at 375** (524 px wide).
   - "Start with Hearing Word Boundaries in Spoken Sentences" is a no-wrap button in a `1fr` grid column, which can't shrink below its content.
   - The columns are now `minmax(0, 1fr)` for `.path-now`, `.review-now` and `.gate-now`.
   - On phones the full-width `.path-actions .btn-lg` may wrap onto two lines.
7. **Header at 768:** "1 day in a row" broke onto two lines. Added `white-space: nowrap` on `.stat-pill`.

### Accessibility
8. **Focus hidden behind bars (WCAG 2.2 2.4.11).** Tabbing on phones landed controls under:
   - the fixed tab bar (every path page: unit toggles, "Start level", "Revisit");
   - the floating save bars (style quiz: 18 of 20 stops; onboarding: 12; survey);
   - at desktop width, the onboarding and style-quiz save bars.

   The fix is `scroll-padding` on `html`:
   - the header height on top;
   - the tab bar, the save bar (sized from the measured 135-141 px plus its 70 px offset) or the player's fixed Next bar at the bottom.

   Result: 103 to 1. The remaining one is the base-lesson error notice, where the wrapped link's midpoint lands on the other link in the same sentence; nothing covers it.
9. **Session recap had no heading at all.** A visually hidden `<h1>Session summary</h1>` was added (`js/session.js`).
10. **Meter values were read as bare numbers.** "0 / 90%" did not say what the 0 was, and a screen reader heard "zero slash ninety percent". `App.tmeter` now shows "0% / 90%" and reads it as "0% of 90%" (counts: "5 of 34"). It is used on the path, review, held, stage-check, stage-left and skill-practice pages.

### Copy (untrue, unclear or system talk)
11. **Age screen:** "One question before you go on" asked two things (date of birth and country). It now says "Two questions before you go on" (`onboarding.html`).
12. **Find my grade with no model:** "The placement quiz needs the model, and no provider is configured right now." Now: "Find my grade is not available for this subject right now. You can still pick a grade and take its placement quiz." That is true: the per-grade quizzes run on the question bank.
13. **Stage check showed build notes to learners.** For example: "Needs: play-outs against perfect defence from the KQK table (staged skills/lib/endgame-tables.js), graded on the server." These now show only with `?debug=1`. The row still says "Not checkable here yet".
14. **Stage left:**
    - "(The gate is in shadow: it is shown here and does not hold you yet.)" became "For now this list is for information only: it does not hold you back yet."
    - "held by the graduation coverage gate, not by this one" became "checked separately, before graduation".
15. **"Give Input"** became "Give feedback": sentence case, and the usual word (`js/feedback.js`).

### Consistency with v2
16. **Em dashes in titles and grade names** (a v2 copy rule). `App.cleanTitle` now shows " — " as a comma (as `gradeLabel` already did): "Final: Grade 2 — Rules Learner" becomes "Final: Grade 2, Rules Learner". Onboarding's grade names and its "We suggested" line use `gradeLabel`.
17. **Onboarding "You are already enrolled in"** ran on as a paragraph of 11 grade names. With more than 3, it now gives the count ("11 grades; they are ticked below"). Each one is already ticked and marked "enrolled" in the list.
18. **Profile:**
    - The boxed stat strip (1 / 122 / 4 in three cards, the v2 "stat banner" tell) became the recap's quiet `.facts` list.
    - "email · joined 10/21/2026" became "email, joined October 21, 2026".
    - The "Track · Grade — band" card title became "Track, Grade, band".
19. **Catch-up label on the dashboard** ("Catch-up 1 of 1") was set in the mono face. Mono is for numbers only, so it is now the text face with tabular figures.

## Checked and left alone
- **Contrast:** every app text and background pair passed at both themes, OKLCH subject colours included.
  - The only failures are the chess board coordinates (1.9-2.0:1, the chess convention), in the chess engine's CSS. Staged.
- **Target size:**
  - The header nav links (23 px tall), footer links (20 px) and dashboard subject-name links (21 px) are under 24 px. They pass 2.5.8 by its spacing exception: no other target sits within a 24 px circle of their centre.
  - Every button is 36 px or more, choices 56 px.
- **Render-blocking:** no page has a blocking script. All scripts sit at the end of `<body>`, and the only head resources are two stylesheets.
- **Reduced motion:** the global `prefers-reduced-motion` rule stops every transition and animation except the spinner. No animation was running on any audited page.
- **Verdict `h4` after the page `h1`** (player, review, landing demo): announced fine. Left as a best-practice note (staged).
- **Not edited, by the rules:**
  - chess and piano engine logic;
  - `services/scorecard/*`;
  - server and API code: compression, the dashboard's request per enrollment, catalogue and path payload sizes are staged with measurements;
  - content strings (raw metric names, stage codes, a recap sentence after a held graduation). Staged.

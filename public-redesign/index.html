How it works

Measured first, then taught.

Most study apps start you at level one or take your word for it. This one measures, so you never grind through what you already know or drown in what you are not ready for.

  1. 01

    Placement

    20 to 50 questions, easiest first, ending just above the level you picked. You get a level and a map of what is missing.

  2. 02

    Learning style

    Optional: twenty forced choices about how you actually study. It changes session length, question format and how much explanation you get.

  3. 03

    Sessions

    Short and mixed: review of what is due, work at your level, and a stretch above it. Press Finish and the tutor analyzes the sitting.

  4. 04

    Checkpoints

    A Master quiz every 25 levels, a Grand Master every 100, and a Graduation exam that certifies the whole track.

Placement, not guesswork

Your starting point is measured from your answers, so the first lesson is the one you need.

It learns how you learn

Every answer records what you got right, how long it took and where the reasoning broke. After a few sessions, pacing and formats are chosen for you.

Review timed to forgetting

Each skill has its own schedule. Something you nearly lost comes back tomorrow; something solid comes back in five weeks.

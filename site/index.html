The same material, rebuilt for how you learn.

Savant is an adaptive learning app, starting with ACT prep. It learns how you study, think and remember, and what you like, then rebuilds any lesson, level or test around you, aiming for better understanding and longer memory in much less time.

Learn the learner first. Then rebuild everything around them.

Savant changes more than the next question. It changes the explanation, the examples, the practice and when each skill comes back.

  1. 1

    It learns the learner.

    An adaptive check finds where you really are. Then every answer and every miss adds to a private model of how you study, think, learn and remember, plus what you like and dislike.

  2. 2

    It writes a spec for you.

    The model becomes a personal learning spec: where to start, what to skip, which mistakes to fix first, which examples to use and what pace to keep.

  3. 3

    It rebuilds the material and checks it.

    Any lesson, level or test is rebuilt to your spec. Each claim goes through a source check before you see it, and where research is mixed or a point is opinion, the lesson says so.

  4. 4

    It times review to your memory.

    Savant tracks how well you remember each skill and schedules it back for when you are likely to start forgetting. A skill that is slipping returns sooner; a solid one waits longer.

Start with the test in front of you.

ACT prep

Sixteen ACT grades, section by section, under the real test's timing. A placement finds your starting point, and the lessons, practice and checkpoints are rebuilt around what you actually miss.

When you miss a checkpoint, Savant works out what you misunderstood and teaches that before you try again.

Your own material

Upload a file, paste a link, add a video or record a lecture. Savant turns it into study material built for you.

Catch up to grade level

Find the gaps under the grade you are in, and close them in order.

Our goal is 3x more efficient learning. We have not measured it yet.

At least 3x, on average, is a goal. The beta is how we find out whether it is true, and for whom.

Built
Nine subjects with 219 grades between them, adaptive placement, per-learner paths, review timed to memory, and a source-checking step for lessons. In testing with a small group.
How we will measure it
In the beta: scores before and after, retention against learners on a path that is not tailored, and the time it takes.
Not built yet
Coding, art, and emotional intelligence and confidence are next. Later: a voice tutor, study groups, iPhone and Android apps, and the app itself in other languages.

Nine subjects built so far.

ACT prep comes first. Eight more subjects are built and in testing alongside it. When Savant is released, it is meant to build a curriculum for any subject you want to learn, with each lesson checked against credible sources.

Each subject is a ladder of grades, from a first look to research level; a grade here is a band of knowledge, not a school year.

Newest. Chess, piano and basketball were added most recently, and some of their hands-on activities are still being finished.

What to expect

An early version. Some things will break, and after levels you will be asked what you liked and what you did not. That feedback is the point of the beta.

Some features send what you write to outside AI services to answer or mark it. The app's privacy policy lists them, and you see it before you start.

Clear rows, then answer to bank their points

Stack the pieces to fill a horizontal row. The game pauses for each question.

← → move · ↓ soft drop · ↑ / X rotate · Z rotate back · Space hard drop · P pause.
Touch controls work too. Each cleared row earns 100 points only if its question is correct. Finish five clears to complete a round; reaching the top ends it early. Play again continues the question pool.

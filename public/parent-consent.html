Your child signed up to Savant, a learning app, and gave this email address as their parent or guardian. Please read what Savant keeps about your child, then decide below.

What you are agreeing to

We got your email address from your child, and we use it only to ask for your consent and, if you agree, to run your parent account. Savant needs your consent before your child can use their account in full. If you do nothing, we delete your email address, your child's account and everything in it after 14 days.

What we collect from your child

  • A first name or nickname, a username and a password (stored scrambled). We do not ask your child for an email address, phone number, home address or photo.
  • Their month and year of birth and their country, from the age question.
  • Their learning record: subjects and levels, answers, scores, time spent on activities, streaks and points, and what they type into lessons (answers, essays, comments about a lesson).
  • Results from activities that use the camera or microphone, such as "shot made" or "notes played correctly". Never pictures, video or sound (see below).
  • One cookie that keeps them signed in. No advertising or analytics cookies.

How we use it

Only to run Savant for your child: to find their level, choose and mark their lessons, show their progress, keep them signed in and keep the service secure. We never sell it, never use it for advertising and never show your child ads.

Camera and microphone

Some basketball and piano activities can use your child's camera or microphone. The camera or microphone is only switched on when your child starts one of those activities and they are processed on your child's device. Savant never uploads, records or keeps pictures, video or sound. We keep only results, such as "shot made" or "notes played correctly", with your child's progress. We do not identify your child from their face or voice. Your child's browser or phone will ask for permission each time or the first time; you can say no and your child can still do the same activities by tapping or using the on-screen keys. You can review and delete your child's results at any time.

Outside AI services: off unless you choose them

Some Savant features send what a learner writes or says to an outside AI company to answer or mark it: the question coach and "ask about this", chat help inside lessons, essay marking, and the Spanish speaking and writing activities. For your child these stay off. Agreeing below does not turn them on. You can choose them separately below; because that means sharing your child's words with another company, it takes a signed form as well (we explain how after you agree). The companies are named in the privacy policy.

Savant does not use children's data to train AI models, its own or anyone else's. You can say now whether you would ever allow it; that also needs a signed form, and we would tell you before starting.

Keeping and deleting

We keep your child's data while their account exists. You can delete the account and everything in it at any time from the parent page, and it is deleted at once. If you withdraw consent, the account is locked straight away and deleted after 30 days unless you give consent again.

Your rights

At any time you can see everything we keep about your child and download it, turn the camera and microphone features off, refuse further collection by withdrawing consent, and have the data deleted. You can agree to Savant collecting and using your child's data without agreeing to any sharing with outside companies. Use the parent page at /parent, or contact us as the privacy policy says.

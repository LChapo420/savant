Chess in Savant: data and programs from others
================================================

Data shipped with the app
-------------------------
Lichess puzzle database (content/chess-puzzles-lichess.js)
  https://database.lichess.org/ - released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0.
  A curated subset, each puzzle re-checked by us. Each puzzle links to its page
  on lichess.org once it has been played. Thank you, Lichess and its players.

Lichess opening names (content/chess-openings-data.js)
  https://github.com/lichess-org/chess-openings - CC0 1.0 ("As a collection of
  facts, this data set is in the public domain").

Programs that run on our servers only (never sent to your browser)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Stockfish 19 - https://stockfishchess.org/ - GNU GPL v3.
  Reviews games and checks puzzles and lessons. It runs as its own program on
  our machines; it is not distributed with or inside this app. Its source code
  is at https://github.com/official-stockfish/Stockfish.

Leorik 3.2 - https://github.com/lithander/Leorik - MIT licence,
  Copyright (c) 2023 Thomas Jahn. A second engine used when Stockfish is absent.

Maia-2 - https://github.com/CSSLab/maia2 - MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2024 CSSLab
  (University of Toronto). Plays the human-like sparring opponent.

Everything else in the chess pages (rules, board, practice bot, lessons, puzzle
trainer, move trainer) is Savant's own code.
